Billing Priority Score Guide

How to explain the queue activity, calculate the score, and coach learners to prioritize claims using operational and financial risk.

i
Recommended learner-facing name: Queue Accuracy Score

The score measures how closely the submitted order matches the expected work queue. It is not a separate weighted risk score.

How the score is calculated

Each claim receives credit only when it is placed in its exact expected position.

Correct positions ÷ 6 claims × 100
67%

Example: Four claims are in their exact correct positions. Four divided by six, rounded to the nearest whole number, equals 67%.

What learners should consider

Priority is based on the combined risk—not on a single data point.

1
Rejection or error statusIs the claim already blocked from adjudication?
2
Timely-filing exposureHow many days remain to correct and transmit?
3
Financial valueHow much reimbursement is at risk?
4
Claim age and readinessCan meaningful action be taken now?

Suggested facilitator explanation

“The Queue Accuracy Score checks whether you arranged the claims according to operational and financial risk. Do not prioritize using only the highest balance or the oldest account. Consider rejection status, timely-filing deadline, balance, claim age, and whether the claim is ready for action.”

Use the score as a coaching signal

A lower score should begin a discussion. Ask the learner to explain why they placed each claim where they did before revealing the expected order.

Give credit for sound reasoning

Exact-position scoring is strict. If two neighboring claims are reversed but the learner identifies the correct risks, recognize the rationale and coach the operational tradeoff.
